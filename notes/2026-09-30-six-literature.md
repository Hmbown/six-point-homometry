# Focused six-point literature search

30 September 2026. No novelty claim. Primary proof dependencies of the
new bounded-torsion and dyad statements are in-house elementary arguments;
unread completeness results are not used as axioms.

## Primary sources actually read

* **Eric Postpischil and Peter Gilbert**, "There Are No New Homometric
  Golomb Ruler Pairs with 12 Marks or Less", *Experimental Mathematics*
  3(2) (1994), 147–152. **VERIFIED**, entire six-page primary
  [PDF](https://emis.de/ft/52010), especially §§3,5–7. §3 gives the
  two-parameter six-mark family; §5 uses symbolic interval matching and
  linear equations, with positivity and collision-free pruning. Its
  published exhaustive computation concerns Golomb rulers; it does not
  classify repeated-distance integer sets or cyclic six-subsets. Symbolic
  matching is prior art, not an invention of this session.
* **Juri Ranieri, Amina Chebira, Yue M. Lu and Martin Vetterli**, "Phase
  Retrieval for Sparse Signals: Uniqueness Conditions", arXiv:1308.3058v2
  (26 September 2013). **VERIFIED for §IV.A / Theorem 1 and its discussion**,
  [primary PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1308.3058). It states the classical
  six-point collision-free parameter family and attributes completeness
  to Bekir–Golomb. It does not reproduce their proof; reading it does not
  make that original proof VERIFIED.
* **G. S. Yovanof and S. W. Golomb**, "The Polynomial Model in the Study
  of Counterexamples to S. Piccard's Theorem", *Ars Combinatoria* 48
  (1998), 43–63. **VERIFIED only pp.43–45** from rendered primary scan:
  [PDF](https://combinatorialpress.com/article/ars/Volume%20048/volume-48-paper-4.pdf).
  The introduction and polynomial model were read; Theorems 1–2 state
  nontrivial factor restrictions and the need for negative coefficients
  in collision-free counterexamples. The remaining proof pages are
  unread, so no whole-paper proof or completeness status is assigned.
* **Robert Morris**, "Z-Related Hexachords explained by Transpositional
  Combination and the Complement Union Property", *Journal MusMat*
  II(2) (December 2018), 79–86. **VERIFIED**, full primary
  [PDF](https://musmat.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/09-z-related-hexachords.pdf),
  §§III–VI. The scope is the fifteen complementary hexachord pairs in
  Z_12. Its five construction cases do not establish arbitrary-n
  six-note completeness.
