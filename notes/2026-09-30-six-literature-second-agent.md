# Second independent six-note literature search

Date searched: 30 September 2026. Status: **[LIT-VERIFY] novelty remains
unestablished**. This is a second live search requested while the symbolic
six-note completeness proof was under final review. The parent agent owns
the parallel required-venue/Patterson search; this pass concentrated on
recent arXiv work and the specified possible overlaps. No accounts, purchases,
author contacts, or paywall bypasses were attempted.

## Findings actually supported by primary sources read

1. **Erickson–Jones, arXiv:2412.08997v4, revised 27 August 2026.**
   The current [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.08997) gives the
   revision date; the [primary HTML, §5](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.08997v4#S5)
   was read, including Problems 5.1 and 5.2. Problem 5.2 explicitly asks for
   the extension to weights k≥6. Its discussion names the fifteen-distance
   factorial obstacle and the extra integer-homometry families appearing at
   six points. Thus the published five-note classification is not itself
   an arbitrary-n six-note result. This is recent evidence about that
   paper's stated frontier, not proof that nobody else has solved it.

2. **Pakin Methawisal, “Unit Actions on Homometric Five-Point Subsets of
   Cyclic Groups: Orbit and fixed-point refinement of the seven-family
   classification,” arXiv:2608.11414v1, 11 August 2026.**
   Read [abstract, introduction and §6](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.11414v1).
   The result refines unit actions on the seven five-point types; §5.4
   transfers these to their complements. The conclusion leaves comparable
   explicit arithmetic parameterizations and unit actions for larger
   cardinalities beyond complementation open. It is directly relevant to
   the distinction between T/I classes and unit orbits, but is not a
   six-point cyclic classification. Full proof was not rechecked here.

3. **Qiuwan Zhao, “Structural and Transformational Relations Between
   Z-Related Hexachords,” MCM 2024, LNCS 14639, 123–134,
   doi:10.1007/978-3-031-60638-0_10.**
   Read the [publisher abstract](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-031-60638-0_10)
   and the author's §4.4 abstract in the
   [primary conference book](https://www.uc.pt/site/assets/files/1724011/mcm_24_e-book.pdf),
   printed pp.39–40 (PDF pp.40–41). Its stated setting is Z12, with [06]
   and [016] subsets, Fourier categorization and dyad expansion. These
   are relevant construction antecedents. **Full chapter remains unread**;
   no claim that our dyad construction is new is justified by this search.

4. **Franck Jedrzejewski, “Finding Homometric Multiplets,” MCM 2024,
   LNCS 14639, 73–84, doi:10.1007/978-3-031-60638-0_6.**
   Read the [publisher abstract and reference list](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-031-60638-0_6)
   and §4.2 of the same conference book, printed pp.37–38. The announced
   classification is of homometric pentachord triples, together with new
   multiplet constructions. It does not announce six-point completeness.
   Publisher exposes subscription preview only; **full proof remains
   [LIT-VERIFY]**. The reference list also points to Jon Wild's 1996
   unpublished enumeration through31; that is an enumeration antecedent,
   not a located general six-point theorem.

5. **Jeremiah Goyette, *The Z-Relation in Theory and Practice*, Rochester
   PhD thesis (2012).**
   The [institutional record](https://urresearch.rochester.edu/institutionalPublicationPublicView.action?institutionalItemId=24384&versionNumber=1)
   and [primary thesis PDF](https://urresearch.rochester.edu/fileDownloadForInstitutionalItem.action?itemFileId=76476&itemId=24908)
   were accessible. Read the abstract, the discussion of crystallographic
   constructions on printed pp.72–76, and the opening of §3.6, printed
   pp.160–161 (PDF pp.176–177). §3.6 explicitly limits the preceding
   algebraic formulas and treats further mod12 hexachords through reciprocal
   set unions without a general variable formula. The thesis is an
   essential source for formula and transformation overlap, including
   pumping. **This was a partial reading**, not an audit of all239 PDF
   pages or proof that no relevant theorem occurs elsewhere in the thesis.

6. **Ranieri–Chebira–Lu–Vetterli, arXiv:1308.3058, §IV.A and Theorem1.**
   Re-read the [primary PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1308.3058), PDF p.5
   (printed p.6). The six-point Bloom parameterization and completeness
   attributed to Bekir are explicitly within the no-repeated-difference
   hypothesis. This source does not establish completeness for repeated
   distances, repeated support atoms, or cyclic six-subsets. Its statement
   also views the Bloom family as permuted two-dimensional linear models,
   an important antecedent for geometric formulations. The original
   Bekir–Golomb proof was not acquired by this pass and stays [LIT-VERIFY].

The most useful new evidence is the **August2026 primary-paper scope**:
the five-note authors still pose the six-and-higher extension, and the
unit-action follow-up treats larger-cardinality arithmetic classification
as open. We did **not locate** a primary arbitrary-n six-point generation
or classification theorem in this pass. That limited result supports
further comparison; it does not certify novelty. Classical construction
antecedents remain substantial, and the inaccessible/partially read
sources above are explicit remaining priority-review obligations.
